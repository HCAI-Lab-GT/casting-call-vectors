Headline (sans mythical)
--------------------------------------------------------
Across 227 roles, the proposed steering vectors occupy
~25.3 effective directions vs ~10.3 for the assistant-axis
family (2.5x more, on unit-normalized vectors), and their mean
pairwise cosine is 0.88 vs 0.97 — i.e. the proposed
vectors are substantially more *directionally* spread out, even though
the assistant-axis vectors have larger individual norms
(9.69 vs 2.67).
