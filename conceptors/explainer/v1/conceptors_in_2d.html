Conceptors in two dimensions

Record a model's internal states while it plays a pirate. That pile of points has a shape. A conceptor is a matrix built from that shape: apply it to a new state and it keeps the parts that point the way pirate states spread, and shrinks the rest. One knob, the aperture, sets how strict it is.

An arrow forgets the shape

A steering vector is the arrow from the average assistant state to the average pirate state. A conceptor starts from the whole pirate cloud: which way it stretches, and how far.

TryStretch and tilt the pirate cloud. Watch the arrow's numbers.

The arrow never moves, so everything you just changed is invisible to a steering vector. Here the arrow is 2 numbers and the shape is a 2×2 table. In Llama‑3‑8B the arrow is 4,096 numbers and the shape is a 4,096×4,096 table.

A conceptor is a soft stencil

Fit it to the pirate cloud and it decides how much of any activation gets through, direction by direction. Directions the cloud uses a lot pass almost untouched. Directions it barely uses get squeezed.

For now the cloud sits at zero. Plate 4 shows why that matters.

Plate 4 of 6

Where the cloud sits changes what it learns

Real activations sit far from zero, and all of them share a large common offset. Fit the conceptor on raw pirate states and its main direction mostly points at where pirate states are. Fit it around the pirate cloud's own center and it learns how pirate states vary.

TrySwitch fits and watch where the grey assistant states land. On the raw fit, raise the gain: they slide along one line toward the pirates.

Plate 5 of 6

Concepts combine like logic

OR leaves room for either concept. AND keeps only the room both share. NOT keeps everything a concept ignores. It is all matrix arithmetic on the stencils, with no new data and no training.

TryPick AND, then turn the doctor cloud until it is perpendicular to the pirate cloud. What happens to the room?

Postmus & Abreu found AND-combined conceptors beat averaged steering vectors on composite tasks. Triantafyllopoulos et al. found AND only helps when the two concepts overlap.

Plate 6 of 6

Check that the stencil is doing the work

After the stencil, the gain β multiplies everything. If every direction ends up multiplied by roughly the same amount, the edit is mostly a zoom and the stencil's shape barely matters.

An October 2026 audit of a re-centered NOT conceptor on persona traits (3 models, 7 traits, aperture 0.05, gain 2) found every direction of every fitted map stretched by at least 1.66×. Removing the matrix while keeping the same center and gain changed many responses, with no consistent extra suppression.

What this means for a pirate persona space

A conceptor has no inside or outside

It edits every activation, including ones already in character (plate 3). The stencil says how much passes in each direction; it never says where the pirate cloud ends.

"Only correct when out of character" is the cap rule. A conceptor could supply the shape that such a rule checks against.

What you fit on is what it means

Pirate states around their own center: how a pirate varies while staying a pirate. Raw pirate states: mostly where pirates sit (plate 4).

Pirate and assistant states pooled: a subspace holding the pirate‑minus‑assistant arrow plus the room around it (the "bipolar" fit in Triantafyllopoulos et al.).

Run the matrix-removed control

Same reference point, same gain, stencil set to zero (plate 6). If behavior barely changes, the shape was not doing the work.

Report the gain per direction alongside the result, so readers can see whether the edit filtered or zoomed.

The math, in six lines
Shape of the cloudR = (1/n) Σ hᵢ hᵢᵀAverage of each activation times itself. Subtract the cloud's center first if you want spread only (plate 4).
The conceptorC = R (R + α⁻² I)⁻¹Along each principal direction with spread λ, it keeps λ / (λ + α⁻²). Always between 0 and 1.
Steer (plain)h′ = β C hPostmus & Abreu 2024. Triantafyllopoulos et al. 2026 also test h′ = (1 − β) h + β C h.
Steer (re-centered)h′ = m + β C (h − m)m is a reference point: a global average (Postmus & Abreu's centered variant) or the concept's own center.
Logic¬C = I − C · C∧B = (C⁻¹ + B⁻¹ − I)⁻¹ · C∨B = conceptor of R_C + R_B
Cap, for comparisonh′ = h + v · max(0, τ − vᵀh)Unit direction v, threshold τ. Moves only states short of the line, and only by the minimum amount.
