Why conceptors don't set it · toy data, real numbers

A conceptor keeps the persona direction, so it can't move you along it

At full strength a centered conceptor maps an activation h to μ + C(h − μ). The gap to the persona center that remains is C(μ − h). If the persona cloud is wide along the persona direction, C keeps that direction, and the gap stays.

Measured on SmolLM3, layer 17, 42,201 assistant tokensAnswer-average conceptor keeps 97% along the persona direction and closes 2.6% of the gap. Per-token conceptor keeps 89% and closes 11%. The steering vector closes all of it by construction.

Measuring it · four judge families

The judges agree on identity and disagree on holding

The standard role-play rubric scores voice, not identity: it calls an answer that admits being an AI "fully in character" as long as it sounds like a pirate. We added an identity rubric and checked every judge against 65 hand-labeled answers.

Takeaways · SmolLM3-3B, pirate persona

Vectors set a persona, conceptors hold one, nothing holds identity

The vector sets it

Of answers fully in character with the steering vector alone at c 1.0, coherence . Conceptors alone barely move it.

The conceptor holds it

Nothing holds identity

Of answers admit being an AI at the turn-8 probe even with prompt + vector, which stays in pirate voice. Every method that keeps coherence admits it.
