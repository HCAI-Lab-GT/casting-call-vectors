Why conceptors don't set it · toy data, real numbers

A conceptor keeps the persona direction, so it can't move you along it

At full strength a centered conceptor maps an activation h to μ + C(h − μ). The gap to the persona center that remains is C(μ − h). If the persona cloud is wide along the persona direction, C keeps that direction, and the gap stays.

Measured on SmolLM3, layer 17, 42,201 assistant tokens, pirate fitsAnswer-average conceptor keeps 97% along the persona direction and closes 2.6% of the gap. Per-token conceptor keeps 89% and closes 11%. The steering vector closes all of it by construction.

Measuring it · four judge families

The judges agree on identity and disagree on holding

The standard role-play rubric scores voice, not identity: it calls an answer that admits being an AI "fully in character" as long as it sounds like the role. We added an identity rubric and checked every judge against 65 hand-labeled answers.
